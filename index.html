Frequently asked questions

Which utilities do you support?

Green Mountain Power today. Vermont Electric Coop, BED, and others coming soon.

How do you get my bills?

A Chrome extension reads them from your existing GMP account. No password sharing. Your data never leaves your browser except as bills sent to our API.

Can I customize the report format?

Standard NEPOOL-GIS format today (pixel-match Bruce Genereaux's master spreadsheet). Custom templates available — contact us.

What happens if I cancel?

Cancel any time from your dashboard. Your data stays accessible for 30 days after.

Do you handle the NEPOOL-GIS IDs?

You enter them once when you add an array. We use them in every report. Auto-pull from GMP doesn't have them — they come from ISO-NE.
