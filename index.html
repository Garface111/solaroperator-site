How it works

1
Install our Chrome extension

One click. We never see your password — we only read the session your browser already holds after you sign in.

2
Paste your activation code

From your welcome email. Click save. The extension is now linked to your Solar Operator workspace.

3
Sign into Green Mountain Power once

Go to greenmountainpower.com and sign in. Check "Stay signed in." Close the tab. That's the whole setup.

4
You're done. Forever.

Around the 20th of each month, we email you your updated reporting spreadsheet. You produce solar; we do the paperwork.
