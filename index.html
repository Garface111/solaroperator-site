Frequently asked questions

Which utilities do you support?

Green Mountain Power today. Vermont Electric Coop, BED, and others coming soon.

How do you get my bills?

Each of your operator clients installs our Chrome extension and logs into their utility account once. We capture bills in the background and aggregate them in your dashboard. You stay in control of communication and reporting.

Can I customize the report format?

Every report ships in the exact layout NEPOOL-GIS accepts for quarterly net-metering credit reporting — one sheet per array, six rolling quarters, standard MWh-to-REC math, and the verbatim NEPOOL footnote. We pixel-matched it against the master spreadsheet a senior Vermont solar operator has used for years. If your downstream consumer needs a different layout, email us — we'll work with you on a custom template.

What happens if I cancel?

Cancel any time from your dashboard. Your data stays accessible for 30 days after.

Do you handle the NEPOOL-GIS IDs?

You enter them once when you add an array. We use them in every report. Auto-pull from your utility portal doesn't have them — they come from ISO-NE.

Do I have to know my array count to sign up?

No. At checkout you choose: enter your exact clients and arrays now to lock in your precise monthly bill, or give a rough estimate and true-up later. If you estimate, your subscription adjusts automatically as you add or remove arrays — you'll always see the change before it hits your card. Either way, you only ever pay for the arrays you actually report on.
